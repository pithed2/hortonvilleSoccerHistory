# De Pere vs. Appleton North — organized scouting notes

**Result:** De Pere 4–0 Appleton North  
**Source:** Andrew Montalbano’s film notes and supplied goal/assist credits.  
**Match date:** Not recorded in the original notes. Clock references below are retained as written.

## Main preparation priorities

1. **Defend the six-yard box on corners.** Three of the four goals came from corners. De Pere repeatedly served into the six, used grouped starting positions and later placed a player on the goalkeeper.
2. **Stay organized through quick restarts.** The other goal came from a quick free kick: #10 Skurka released #7 Franda on the left before North was set.
3. **Attack the space behind the fullbacks.** Quick switches created North’s attacking opportunities in the reviewed passages. Central dribbling repeatedly failed.
4. **Control second balls and Skurka’s movement.** #8 Thiem takes long goal kicks; #10 checks toward the ball and then attacks forward aggressively.

These priorities are coaching recommendations drawn from the observations below.

## Possession and attacking movement

- **Skurka (#10):** Repeatedly comes back toward the ball, then attacks forward hard. His movement needs coverage both before and after he releases it.
- **Midfield:** Frequent positional interchange. Shanda is observed centrally in this match.
- **Fullbacks:** Push high, particularly Lofdahl (#12). At times, the two center backs are left behind the attack.
- **Wide midfielders:** The notes describe limited defensive recovery.

**Coaching application:** Agree who steps to Skurka and who covers his next run. On regains, look early behind an advanced fullback; support the receiver rather than relying on an isolated runner.

### Free kicks

- **Longer free kicks:** Thiem (#8) takes them, allowing midfielders to move into attacking positions.
- **Quick attacking free kick:** Four players gather at the ball; Skurka finds Franda wide left for goal 3.

**Coaching application:** Keep attention on the ball and wide runners while the team sets. A group discussing the restart does not mean the restart will be delayed.

### Goal kicks and kickoff

- Thiem (#8), rather than Baumgardt, takes the observed goal kicks. All are long; the first reaches halfway.
- At kickoff, five players begin high and four in the back line. The ball goes to a lone central midfielder, then aerially into the center to contest the second ball.

**Coaching application:** Organize the first aerial challenge and supporting second-ball positions separately.

### Defensive corners

- De Pere leaves **two players up front**.

**Coaching application:** Account for both outlets before deciding how many players to commit to our attacking corner.

## Transition opportunities

**Observed:** Quick switches aimed at catching the advanced fullbacks produced North’s only offense in the reviewed passages. Attempts to dribble through the center failed almost every time.

**Proposed approach:** Move the ball early toward the space behind a fullback, or switch quickly before the weak side recovers. Confirm the space is actually available rather than forcing the pass. Keep recovery cover if the first forward ball is lost.
